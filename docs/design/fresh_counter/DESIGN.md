## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the warmth, hospitality, and swift momentum of an artisanal office food hall. Moving away from cold corporate tech aesthetics, it treats workplace dining as a rejuvenating culinary break. 

The aesthetic is anchored in **Fresh Counter Modernism**—combining inviting warm cream foundations, crisp ceramic white cards, and appetizing terracotta/burnt orange accents. It balances tactile culinary warmth with rapid transactional utility, ensuring lunch rushes, desk-side pre-orders, and counter pickup queues flow with effortless clarity.

### Personality & Core Attributes
- **Appetizing & Inviting:** Rich culinary terracottas paired with toasted espresso typography stimulate hunger, hospitality, and comfort.
- **Fast-Casual Clarity:** Clean, elevated card architecture separates dishes, meal deals, and quick-add actions to minimize cognitive strain during peak lunch hours.
- **Regulatory Integrity:** Rigorous adherence to standardized Indian FSSAI dietary markers (Veg green dot vs. Non-veg brown triangle) guarantees instant, trusted identification across diverse workplace diets.

## Colors

The palette establishes an earthy, appetizing culinary hierarchy grounded in warm, low-fatigue tones:

### Surface & Canvas
- **App Canvas (`surface-cream`):** `#FBF6EF` produces an organic, welcoming bakery-and-kitchen ambiance.
- **Card & Active Containers (`surface-card` & `surface-card-warm`):** Pure `#FFFFFF` and crisp warm off-white `#FFFDF9` lift items cleanly off the canvas.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Soft warm sand `#F3E8DC` for gentle element division and `#EAD7C5` for input boundaries and selected card states.

### Primary Accents & Hierarchy
- **Deep Terracotta (`primary`):** `#C2410C` serves as the primary anchor for CTAs, checkout buttons, active segmented tabs, and cart badges.
- **Vibrant Orange (`secondary`):** `#EA580C` powers dynamic accents, counter highlights, promo tags, and interactive hover feedback.
- **Text Primary:** Deep espresso charcoal `#2B211B` delivers high-contrast, comfortable readability without the starkness of neutral black.
- **Text Secondary:** Roasted mocha `#6E5D53` provides clear secondary hierarchy for dish descriptions, weights, and pickup timestamps.

### Regulatory Dietary Identity (FSSAI)
- **Veg Indicator:** `#15803D` solid dot encased in a matching square border on `#F0FDF4`.
- **Non-Veg Indicator:** `#7C2D12` solid triangle encased in a matching square border on `#FEF2F2`.

## Typography

The type system blends friendly, geometric clarity with rigid transactional functionality:

1. **Plus Jakarta Sans** guides general UI storytelling, menu browsing, nutrition disclosures, and header displays. Its soft rounded forms evoke a warm, casual food-counter feel while retaining crisp corporate legibility.
2. **JetBrains Mono** is preserved for mission-critical fulfillment mechanics: queue numbers (`#A-34`), order pickup codes, pricing tables, kitchen dispatch timers, and receipt stamps. Monospaced rendering prevents layout shifts during live counter countdowns.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured fluid layout is governed by an 8pt base grid with 4pt half-steps for compact counter tickets and ingredient badges.

### Responsive Breakpoints
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins and `0.75rem` gutters. Food catalog scrolls vertically with a persistent bottom floating cart tray.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins and `1rem` gutters. Accommodates two-column order flow or canteen self-service kiosk displays.
- **Desktop (1025px+):** 12-column layout with a maximum container of `1280px`, `2rem` margins, and `1.5rem` gutters. Displays dual-panel menu browsing alongside live office order status feeds.

## Elevation & Depth

Warm lighting and tactile physical surfaces inspire the elevation model:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#FBF6EF` warm cream base.
- **Level 1 (Menu Cards & Counters):** `#FFFFFF` or `#FFFDF9` surface with a 1px border of `#F3E8DC`. Shadow: `0px 2px 4px rgba(43, 33, 27, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Food Highlights):** 1px border `#EAD7C5` with a warm diffused shadow: `0px 8px 16px -2px rgba(194, 65, 12, 0.08), 0px 3px 6px -1px rgba(43, 33, 27, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Cart Tray, Popovers & Dialogs):** `#FFFFFF` surface, 1px border `#F3E8DC`, with deep ambient grounding: `0px 16px 24px -4px rgba(43, 33, 27, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a gentle, rounded aesthetic (`roundedness: 2`) that feels soft, approachable, and tactile:

- **Cards & Dialog Sheets (`1rem` / `rounded-lg`):** Generous rounded corners give menu cards a friendly, appetizing feel.
- **Buttons, Steppers & Form Inputs (`0.5rem` / `rounded`):** Balanced curvature ensures buttons feel click-friendly and distinct from surrounding content.
- **Chips, Diet Badges & Scarcity Pills (`9999px` / `rounded-full`):** Complete pill curves for status markers, dietary tags, and live quantity counters.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Checkout):** Deep Terracotta `#C2410C` background, `#FFFFFF` text. Hover: `#EA580C`. Active: `#9A3412`. Height: 44px for touch targets. Border-radius: 8px. Typography: `label-lg`.
- **Secondary Action (View Ingredients / Repeat Order):** `#FFFFFF` background, 1.5px border `#EAD7C5`, `#2B211B` text. Hover: `#FBF6EF` background with `#C2410C` text.
- **Quantity Stepper (`- [ qty ] +`):** Pill shape with `#F3E8DC` background, `#2B211B` numerals in `ticket-code-sm`, and `#C2410C` icons.

### 2. Dietary Markers (FSSAI Regulatory Standards)
- **Veg Marker:** 16x16px square container with 2px border-radius and 1.5px solid border in `#15803D`. Internal centered dot (6px diameter) in `#15803D`. Background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Non-Veg Marker:** 16x16px square container with 2px border-radius and 1.5px solid border in `#7C2D12`. Internal centered equilateral triangle (7px base & height) in `#7C2D12`. Background `#FFFFFF`.

### 3. Food Catalog Cards
- **Container:** `#FFFFFF` or `#FFFDF9` surface, 1px solid border `#F3E8DC`, 16px corner radius.
- **Image Treatment:** Rounded 12px thumbnail with a soft warm border ring.
- **Header & Price:** Item name in `headline-sm` (`#2B211B`), price in `ticket-code-sm` (`#C2410C`).
- **Scarcity Tag ("Only 3 left"):** Pill badge with `#FEF3C7` background, 1px solid `#FDE68A` border, and `#B45309` text.

### 4. Kitchen & Pickup Ticket
- **Container:** `#FFFDF9` background, 1.5px solid `#EAD7C5` border, dashed inner separator separating the order receipt and token header.
- **Pickup Code:** `ticket-code-lg` rendered in deep espresso `#2B211B`.
- **Live State:** Ready status signaled by `#F0FDF4` badge with `#15803D` bold text.

### 5. Input Fields
- **Container:** Height 44px, 8px radius, `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid border `#EAD7C5`.
- **Focus:** 1.5px border `#C2410C` with a 3px ring in `#F3E8DC`.
- **Placeholder:** `#6E5D53` at 60% opacity.

### 6. Chips & Filters
- **Default:** `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#F3E8DC` border, `#6E5D53` text.
- **Selected:** Terracotta fill `#C2410C`, `#FFFFFF` text, zero border.
