# Stage 5e — UI Design Requirements (design brief)

**Product:** CanteenQ — Office Canteen Pre-order Web App
**Purpose of this document:** Everything a designer (or a design tool) needs to design the full UI of CanteenQ.
**Version:** 1.1 · **Date:** November 2026 · **Owner:** _<your name>_

> **Outcome:** this brief was given to the Stitch AI design tool, which produced the **"Fresh Counter"** design system and screens S3, S4, S5 and S10 (see `docs/design/`). Fresh Counter is a refined version of style **A** below, using **Plus Jakarta Sans** + **JetBrains Mono** and Material Symbols icons. Styles B and C are kept here only as the record of the options considered. Design details that Stitch added beyond this brief, and the decisions taken on them, are listed in `02-requirements.md §6`.

---

## 1. Product summary

CanteenQ lets office employees **order lunch from the canteen before they get there**. They choose dishes from today's menu before a cut-off time (11:00), pick a 15-minute pickup slot, and get a **QR code**. At the counter, staff scan the QR code and hand over the food. The canteen manager sets the daily menu and quantities and sees a dashboard. An admin manages users and settings.

**The one-line goal for the design:** *Ordering lunch should take under a minute, and picking it up should take under 30 seconds.*

## 3. Design goals and principles

1. **Mobile-first for employees and staff.** Design at 390 px wide first, then scale up.
2. **Time is always visible.** The cut-off countdown and the pickup slot must be easy to see on every ordering screen.
3. **Scarcity is honest.** Always show "N left" and "Sold out". Never let users add something that's unavailable.
4. **Counter screens are readable from arm's length:** large type, high contrast, one main action per screen.
5. **Errors explain what to do next.** Example: "Ordering for today closed at 11:00 — order for tomorrow instead."
6. **No payment step.** Orders are added to a monthly statement. Say this in a small line at checkout.
7. **Calm and trustworthy, not flashy.** No gradients for decoration and no emoji as icons.

### 4.4 Spacing, sizes, icons
- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40 px.
- **Minimum tap target: 44 × 44 px.** Counter buttons are at least 56 px tall.
- Icons: simple line icons (2 px stroke), for example Lucide or Heroicons. Icon-only buttons need a text label for screen readers.
- Images: no food photos in the MVP. Use a coloured tile with the dish initials (e.g. "VB"), so the layout works without photos.

## 7. Screen-by-screen requirements

### S1 · Login
- **Content:** app name/logo, email field, password field (with show/hide), "Log in" button, "Create an account" link.
- **States:**
  - Wrong details: *"Invalid email or password."* (same message for an unknown email and a wrong password)
  - Locked: *"Too many attempts. Try again in 15 minutes."*
  - Disabled account: *"Your account is disabled. Contact the admin."*
  - Session expired (banner): *"Your session expired. Please log in again."*
  - Loading: the button shows a spinner and is disabled.
- **After login:** Employee → Menu; Staff → Queue; Manager → Dashboard; Admin → Users.

### S2 · Sign up
- **Fields:** full name, company email, password (with rules shown under the field), confirm password.
- **Inline errors (shown before submitting):**
  - *"Use your company email (@rubicotech.com or @rubicotech.in)"*
  - *"Password must be 8–64 characters with at least one letter and one number"*
  - *"An account with this email already exists"*
- **Success:** go straight to the Menu.

### S3 · Today's menu (Employee, mobile) — main screen
- **Header:** greeting or app name, profile button.
- **Day switch:** two tabs, **Today** and **Tomorrow** (only these two).
- **Cut-off banner:** *"Order before 11:00 · 42 min left"* plus the pickup window *"12:30–14:30"*.
- **Filters:** All / Veg / Non-veg (and optional categories: Mains, Sides, Drinks).
- **Dish card (repeat):** veg/non-veg marker, dish name, short description, price (₹), stock ("12 left", or "Only 3 left" in the warning colour), and an **Add** button that becomes a **– 1 +** stepper (max 5).
- **Sold-out card:** greyed out, "Sold out" badge, no Add button.
- **Cart bar (stays at the bottom when the cart has items):** item count, total, "Checkout →".
- **Bottom navigation:** Menu · My orders · Profile.
- **Other states:**
  - After cut-off: banner *"Ordering for today closed at 11:00"* and a button to switch to Tomorrow.
  - Menu not published: *"Today's menu isn't published yet. Check back soon."*
  - Canteen closed (weekend/holiday): *"The canteen is closed today."*
  - Already ordered today: banner *"You already have an order for today"* with a link to view it.
  - Loading: skeleton cards.

### S4 · Checkout — pick a slot (Employee, mobile)
- **Can be** a full screen or a bottom sheet over the menu.
- **Content:** order summary (items × qty, prices, total), note *"Added to your monthly canteen statement"*.
- **Slot picker:** a grid of 15-minute slot buttons (12:30 … 14:15). Selected = accent colour; full = disabled with "Full".
- **Button:** "Place order · ₹120". Disabled until a slot is chosen; shows a spinner while sending (stops double taps).
- **Errors shown inline (above the button):**
  - *"Only 1 Veg Biryani left"* (with a fix: reduce the quantity)
  - *"Ordering for today closed at 11:00"*
  - *"This slot just filled up. Pick another."*

### S5 · Order confirmed / My order + QR (Employee, mobile)
- **Top:** pickup date and slot, status badge.
- **QR code:** large, centred, at least 180 px, on a white background even in dark styles.
- **Pickup code:** 6 characters in large monospace text, e.g. **K7Q4MZ**, for when scanning doesn't work.
- **Status tracker:** Placed → Preparing → Ready → Collected (current step highlighted).
- **When READY:** a clear green message: *"Your lunch is ready. Show this code at the counter."*
- **Order details:** items, quantities, total.
- **Cancel button:** only before the cut-off. A confirmation dialog: *"Cancel this order? The items go back on the menu."*
- **After the cut-off:** note *"Ordering is closed, so this order can no longer be cancelled."*

### S6 · My orders — history (Employee)
- A list, newest first: date, items, total, final status (Collected / Cancelled / No-show) as a coloured badge.
- "Load more" (10 per page).
- **Empty state:** *"No orders yet"* plus a "Go to menu" button.

### S7 · Staff order queue (Staff/Manager, tablet first)
- **Top:** date, status filter chips with counts (All, Placed 40, Preparing 8, Ready 12), a big **Scan / Enter code** button.
- **Orders grouped by pickup slot** (12:30–12:45, 12:45–13:00, …), with an order count per group. Highlight the current slot.
- **Order card:** pickup code, employee name, items, status badge, and one action button:
  - PLACED → **Start**
  - PREPARING → **Ready**
  - READY → no button (it waits for the scan)
- **Cancelled orders:** greyed out, struck through, no buttons.
- **Tablet:** 2 slot columns side by side. **Mobile:** one column.

### S8 · Scan / verify pickup (Staff, mobile/tablet) — counter screen
- **Camera viewfinder** with corner guides and the text *"Point at the QR code"*.
- **Manual entry:** a large code input (monospace, upper-case, 6 characters) with a "Find" button.
- **Result card (success):** code, status, employee name, slot, items, and a **very large "HAND OVER" button** (at least 56 px tall).
- **Warning (order not marked ready):** *"Not marked ready yet"*. Staff can still hand over.
- **Error messages (large, red, with an icon):**
  - *"Already collected at 13:04"*
  - *"This order is for 18 Nov, not today"*
  - *"This order was cancelled"*
  - *"Code not found"*
- **After hand-over:** a big green tick, *"Handed over"*, then the screen resets for the next person.

### S9 · Prep list (Staff/Manager)
- A big, simple table or tiles: dish name and total portions to cook (cancelled orders not counted).
- Readable from 2 metres away (numbers 36 px or more). A print button is optional.

### S10 · Manager dashboard (desktop)
- **Top:** page title, date picker, "Prep list" button, "Export CSV" button.
- **KPI tiles (6):** Total orders, Revenue (₹), Collected, Pending, Cancelled, No-show.
- **"Items to prepare":** dish, ordered / limit, progress bar; "Sold out" tag when full.
- **"Orders by pickup slot":** a bar chart (or horizontal bars) per 15-minute slot; highlight the busiest slot.
- **Empty state:** tiles show 0 and the charts say *"No orders for this date"*.
- **Admin view:** the same screen, read-only (no edit actions).

### S11 · Menu items — catalogue (Manager)
- Table: name, category, veg/non-veg, price, status (Active/Archived), Edit action.
- Search box, category filter, "Add item" button.
- Archived items: greyed, hidden by default (toggle "Show archived").

### S12 · Add / edit menu item (Manager)
- **Fields:** name (max 80), description (max 300), category (Main / Side / Dessert / Beverage), price ₹ (1–1000, up to 2 decimals), diet (Veg / Non-veg / Jain), active toggle.
- **Errors:** *"Price must be between ₹1 and ₹1,000"*, *"An item with this name already exists"*.
- Buttons: Save, Cancel. Archive is a secondary, low-emphasis action.

### S13 · Daily menu editor (Manager)
- **Date picker** (today or future dates only).
- **List of active dishes**, each with an "On the menu" checkbox and a **quantity** field (1–1000).
- Show "already ordered" next to each dish for today; block lowering the quantity below that number: *"Can't be lower than already ordered (25)"*.
- Buttons: "Copy from yesterday" (optional), **Save menu**.
- A past date is read-only.

### S14 · Users management (Admin)
- Table: name, email, role (dropdown: Employee, Staff, Manager, Admin), status (Active/Disabled toggle), joined date.
- Search, role filter, pagination (20 per page).
- Confirm before changing a role or disabling an account.
- Error: *"At least one active admin is required."*

### S15 · Canteen settings (Admin)
- **Fields:** order cut-off time, pickup window start and end, slot length (10 / 15 / 20 / 30 min), slot capacity (optional), max quantity per item, allowed email domains, closed weekdays (checkboxes), holiday dates (list with add/remove).
- **Errors:** *"Pickup window end must be after start"*, *"Cut-off must be before the first pickup slot"*.
- Note under the form: *"Changes apply to new orders only."*

### S16 · Exports (Manager/Admin)
- Two cards: **Orders (date range)** and **Monthly statement (per employee)**.
- Each has date or month pickers and a "Download CSV" button.
- Error: *"Date range must be 1–92 days."*

### S17 · Not authorised / Not found
- A friendly message, a short explanation and a "Go to my home" button.

### S18 · Emails (optional)
- **Order confirmed:** items, slot, pickup code (large), and the cancel deadline.
- **Order ready:** *"Your lunch is ready — pickup 13:00–13:15"*, plus the code.

## 9. Accessibility requirements

- Text contrast at least **4.5:1** (3:1 for text 24 px and larger).
- Tap targets at least **44 × 44 px**.
- Never rely on colour alone (veg markers use shape; statuses use text labels).
- All form fields have visible labels; errors sit next to the field.
- Visible focus outline on everything that can be selected with the keyboard.
- The QR code always has the text code next to it.
- Respect the system "reduce motion" setting; keep animation minimal.

## 11. Out of scope for design

- Online payment, wallet or card screens
- Food photos or ratings (later)
- Native app-store designs (this is a responsive web app)
- Multiple canteens or vendors
- Dark mode for every screen (only the staff counter may use the dark style C)
